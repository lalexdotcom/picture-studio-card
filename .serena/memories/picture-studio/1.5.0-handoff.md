## The lesson of walk fixes 9–13, worth not repeating

Five consecutive fixes in one area, and the first four defects were mine. The
common cause was **reasoning about expressions instead of dependencies**:

1. The refusal rule reached two of its three call sites (`_formTarget` missed).
2. Unifying all four call sites erased a distinction: the editor asks "is this
   item broken", the card asks "would Home Assistant otherwise draw this
   successfully". **Factoring an identical boolean is not factoring an identical
   question.**
3. The card was moved onto a probe-dependent predicate without anything on the
   card side starting a probe — inert on a real dashboard, which is where it
   mattered.
4. Then the `type: "error"` construction itself turned out to be unreliable.

Every one compiled, passed a green suite, and survived review. **Only the user's
browser walk caught them.** Reviews confirm that what they are shown is
consistent; they do not tell you what is missing around it.
